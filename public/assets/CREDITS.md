## Solar System imagery and stars (spec 022)

Equirectangular maps, fetched by `scripts/fetch-solar-imagery.mjs` (sources and treatments in
`assets-src/solar-system/sources.json`), brought to 0° longitude at the centre, sized 2K (planets) or 1K, and
encoded to KTX2 by `npm run assets`. Greyscale maps are tinted by the body's colour (D-031).
